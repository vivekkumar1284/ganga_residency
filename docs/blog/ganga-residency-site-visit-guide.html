1. Driving Routes & Travel Times from Major NCR Hubs

Driving down to garhmukteshwar via NH-9 (Delhi-Lucknow Highway) is a smooth, high-speed journey. The road consists of a multi-lane signal-free stretch up to Hapur, followed by smooth express corridors right to the township entrance.

Starting Location Recommended Route Distance (Approx.) Average Drive Time
East / Central Delhi (Akshardham / Laxmi Nagar) NH-9 via Delhi-Meerut Expressway -> Dasna -> Hapur Bypass -> garhmukteshwar 82 km 70 - 80 mins
Noida & Greater Noida (Sector 62 / Pari Chowk) Noida-Greater Noida Link Rd -> NH-9 via Ghaziabad -> Hapur -> garhmukteshwar 75 km 65 - 75 mins
Ghaziabad (Indirapuram / Raj Nagar Extension) Direct NH-9 Highway -> Dasna Interchange -> garhmukteshwar Toll 58 km 50 - 60 mins
Meerut City Meerut-garhmukteshwar Road (NH-334) -> Salarpur / Brijghat Crossing 42 km 45 mins

2. Reaching via Train, Bus, and Rail Options

If you prefer public transportation for your preliminary visit, garhmukteshwar is exceptionally well-connected by Northern Railways and UPSRTC interstate bus fleets:

Express Trains

Board trains heading towards Moradabad/Bareilly from Anand Vihar Terminal, Old Delhi, or Nizamuddin stations. Alight at garhmukteshwar (GMS) Station or garhmukteshwar Bridge (GMB) Station. The site is a quick 10-minute auto ride away.

UPSRTC Bus Service
