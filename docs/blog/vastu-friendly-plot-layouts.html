1. Ideal Plot Shapes: Square & Rectangular (Gaumukhi)

The shape of the plot forms the baseline of Vastu Shastra. Irregular or skewed plot shapes disrupt natural energy lines (Prana). Here is what you should evaluate:

  • Rectangular Plots (Ratio 1:1.5 or 1:2): Highly recommended. Promotes balanced growth, peace of mind, and financial stability.
  • Square Plots: Considered the most auspicious. Distributes cosmic energy uniformly across all four corners.
  • Gaumukhi Plots (Narrow front, wide back): Excellent for residential living. Symbolizes accumulation of wealth, safety, and family security.
  • Avoid Cuts & Sharp Corners: Steer clear of triangular, L-shaped, or irregular multi-corner plots with missing corners in the North-East (Ishan) zone.

Corner Plots (Two or Three Side Open): Plots with roads on two sides—especially East and North—are considered extremely favorable.
