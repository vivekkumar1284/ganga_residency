1. Core Land Title & Ownership Documents

The foundation of any secure real estate investment lies in undisputed land titles. Always examine these primary records before committing funds:

Sale Deed (Kraya Patra)

The definitive legal instrument transferring ownership from the developer or landowner to the buyer, registered at the local sub-registrar office.

Khatauni (Land Record Extract)

Official revenue department document displaying the continuous chain of ownership, survey numbers, and land parcel shareholders.

Encumbrance Certificate (EC)

Crucial proof confirming that the land parcel is free from any legal monetary liabilities, pending loans, or mortgage disputes.

Mutation Record (Dakhil Kharij)

Government record reflecting the transfer of property title in municipal revenue registers after purchase execution.

2. Statutory Approvals & Regulatory Compliance

Plotted developments must comply with state urban planning laws and local body regulations. Ensure the following approvals are verified:

Section 143 Land Conversion Certificate

Validates that the agricultural land has been legally converted to residential use under UP land reform acts, ensuring lawful habitation.

Approved Master Layout Plan

Sanctioned drawing detailing plot numbers, internal roads, green parks, and utility corridors, viewable on our Master Plan Page.

Environmental & Local Body Clearances

NOCs from local development authorities ensuring the project adheres to environmental and regional zoning standards.
