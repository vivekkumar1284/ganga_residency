2. Token Booking & Allotment Letter Issuance

The plot possession process begins when a buyer chooses a specific plot unit on the layout plan. At Ganga Residency, buyers can lock in pre-launch or current release rates by submitting a booking form alongside mandatory KYC documents (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, and passport-size photographs).

Upon receipt of the initial token payment (typically 10% to 20% of the total plot cost), the project management team issues a formal **Allotment Letter**. This legal document specifies:

  • Unique Plot Identification: Block number, Plot number, and precise square yardage.

  • Payment Milestone Schedule: Clear dates for installments, registry execution, and physical handover.
  • Price Protection Guarantee: Clause locking the per-square-yard price against future market price revisions. (See: Best Time to Book a Pre-Launch Plot).

4. Registry & Stamp Duty Execution in UP

The sale deed execution (commonly referred to as *Registry*) transfers direct legal ownership of the freehold land from the developer to the buyer. In Uttar Pradesh, registry requires stamp duty payment calculated based on the prevailing circle rate or agreement value.

Key Steps in Registry Execution:

  1. Drafting the Sale Deed: Prepared by legal counsel containing exact plot coordinates, boundaries (North, South, East, West neighbor details), and consideration amount.
  2. E-Stamp Purchase & Registration Fee Payment: Paid via official UP treasury portal.
  3. Sub-Registrar Office Appearance: Both buyer and seller (or authorized representative) present biometric authentication and signatures at the Sub-Registrar office in garhmukteshwar.
  4. Receipt of Original Sale Deed: Handed over after official indexing by revenue officers.

5. On-Site Physical Demarcation Process

Having legal title on paper is only half the battle; physical possession requires exact ground measurement. At Ganga Residency Overview, physical demarcation is executed using high-precision Total Station optical survey tools to avoid even an inch of discrepancy.

During the official demarcation date, the buyer is invited to the project site where civil surveyors:

  • Locate the master baseline markers established across the township layout.
  • Measure road widths (wide 30-ft and 40-ft internal asphalt avenues) and park setbacks.
  • Fix temporary steel pins or concrete corner pillars at all four corners of your plot.

6. Boundary Wall Construction & Civil Handover

The final physical milestone of the plot possession process is the construction of a permanent boundary wall. At Ganga Residency, gated township standards mandate neat brickwork or precast concrete boundary demarcation for every handed-over unit.

What is included in Boundary Wall Handover?

  • Substantial Footing & Masonry: Reinforced brick/concrete base raised 2 to 3 feet above road level to prevent soil erosion and water logging.
  • Corner Identification Plaque: Stainless steel or painted stone plaque displaying your official Plot Number.
  • Utility Access Points: Marked conduit points for underground water connection, electricity cabling, and drainage line integration built under Ganga Residency Township Infrastructure.
  • Possession Certificate Sign-Off: A formal document signed by both the customer and engineering team declaring zero survey errors and successful physical handover.
