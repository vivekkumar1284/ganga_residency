Why Suburban Corridors Outperform Core Cities

Peripheral corridors benefit from lower entry ticket sizes combined with high percentage appreciation ceilings. As civic infrastructure, municipal amenities, and expressways expand outward, early investors capture exponential equity gains that mature urban areas can no longer match.
