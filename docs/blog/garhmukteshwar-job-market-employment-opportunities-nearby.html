Uttar Pradesh has witnessed unprecedented economic momentum over recent years. Government-led infrastructure pushes, industrial corridors, and highway expansions have decentralized employment away from congested metropolitan centers like Delhi-NCR, creating thriving sub-urban commercial hubs.
