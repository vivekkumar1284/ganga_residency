Vastu Shastra Significance: East vs North Orientation

Traditional Vedic architecture prioritizes solar energy flow and magnetic orientation. In Western UP real estate markets, both East and North facing plots command high demand due to their auspicious Vastu associations:

East-Facing Plots (Indra Zone)

Associated with the Sun god (Surya) and Lord Indra, representing growth, health, and enlightenment. Captures gentle early-morning sunlight, heating the home naturally while keeping rooms cooler during afternoon hours. Ideal for main entrances and prayer rooms facing East.

North-Facing Plots (Kubera Zone)

Governed by Lord Kubera, the deity of wealth, and Mercury (Budha), associated with commerce and prosperity. Highly prized by business owners and investors. Provides balanced, non-direct sunlight throughout the day, minimizing indoor heat build-up during summers.

Buyers evaluating plot options like 100 sq. yd. vs 150 sq. yd. plots often select East or North orientations to simplify planning for Vastu-compliant main doors, kitchens (South-East), and master bedrooms (South-West).

The Premium Advantage of Corner Plots

Corner plots at Ganga Residency occupy prime locations within the township layout. Bounded by dual wide asphalt roads, corner units offer distinct operational and financial advantages over regular plots:

  • Dual Access Points: Opportunity to create separate vehicular gates or dedicated entry points for family members and guests.
  • Enhanced Natural Lighting & Airflow: Openings on two sides maximize cross-ventilation, reducing reliance on artificial lighting and air conditioning.
  • Architectural Elevation Impact: Corner villas display dual grand facades, enhancing curb appeal and architectural impact within the community.
  • Higher Resale Value: Corner plots consistently attract a 10% to 15% price premium over standard facing plots due to limited inventory.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are East and North facing plots more expensive at Ganga Residency?

Standard East and North-facing plots are generally priced at regular township base rates. However, Corner plots or units facing wide main boulevard roads may carry a standard Preferential Location Charge (PLC).

Which plot orientation is best for maximum resale value?

Corner plots typically generate the highest percentage appreciation over time. Among standard plots, North-facing and East-facing units offer the highest liquidity and fastest buyer turnaround in the secondary market.

Can West or South facing plots be made Vastu-compliant?

Yes. Proper architectural design—such as placing the main entrance in favorable padas along the North-West or South-East—ensures Vastu compliance across all orientations. Detailed guidance is available in our Vastu Plot Layouts Guide.
