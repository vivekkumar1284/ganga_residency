1. What is the Uttar Pradesh Land Ceiling Act?

The Uttar Pradesh Imposition of Ceiling on Land Holdings Act was originally enacted to prevent the concentration of agricultural and rural land in the hands of a few private entities, ensuring equitable distribution. The statute sets strict upper limits (ceilings) on the quantity of land an individual, family, or corporate body can legally own within the state.

However, for urban and urbanizing peripheries—such as garhmukteshwar under regional master plans—agricultural land undergoes legal transformation (commonly referred to as Section 143 / Section 80 conversions under the UP Revenue Code). Once converted into non-agricultural residential use, land is developed into gated communities like Ganga Residency, completely exempting individual plot buyers from rural ceiling restrictions.
