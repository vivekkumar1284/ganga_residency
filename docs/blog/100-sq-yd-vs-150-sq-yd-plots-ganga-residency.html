1. Construction & Layout Capability

The architectural potential of your land depends on its footprint. Here is how both plot options compare for building your home:

  • 100 Sq. Yd. (900 Sq. Ft.): Highly efficient for compact, modern duplexes. Building across Ground + 1 floors allows you to easily create a functional 3 BHK floor plan with a covered porch and terrace space.
  • 150 Sq. Yd. (1,350 Sq. Ft.): Offers 50% more ground coverage. This extra footprint lets you design a spacious living room, open kitchen, dedicated servant/store room, and a private green lawn alongside parking space. Review layout guidelines on the Ganga Residency Master Plan Page.

4. Financial Strategy & ROI Analysis

From an investment standpoint, both plot sizes offer distinct advantages depending on your strategy:

  • For Pure Investors (100 Sq. Yd.): Requires lower capital outlay (₹16.50 Lakhs at pre-launch), making it easier to hold multiple units or exit quickly for capital appreciation as expressway traffic scales up.
  • For End-Users & Second Home Buyers (150 Sq. Yd.): Offers greater long-term usability. A larger villa footprint commands higher rental yield and resale premiums from families seeking spacious weekend homes near the Ganga river basin.
