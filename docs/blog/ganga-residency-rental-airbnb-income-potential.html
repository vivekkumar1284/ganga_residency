2. Monetization Models: Long-Term Rental vs. Short-Term Airbnb

Property owners at Ganga Residency have multiple pathways to monetize their constructed villas or builder floors. Choosing the right model depends on your personal management bandwidth and financial goals:

Revenue Parameter Long-Term Residential Rental Short-Term Airbnb / Homestay
Target Audience Local professionals, teachers, bank employees, or retired families NCR weekend travelers, pilgrims, remote workers, and wedding groups
Income Predictability Fixed monthly rental with stable 11-month lease agreements Variable weekly/monthly income; higher peaks during festival seasons
Average Yield Potential Moderate (3% to 5% annual rental yield on property value) High (8% to 12% annual yield with professional property management)
Management Effort Low maintenance once leased out to reliable tenants Higher requirement for housekeeping, guest check-ins, and upkeep
