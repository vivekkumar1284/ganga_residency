The Delhi-Meerut RapidX (RRTS) Revolution Explained

The Delhi-Ghaziabad-Meerut RRTS line is India’s first state-of-the-art regional rapid transit corridor designed specifically to bridge the commuting gap between National Capital Delhi and eastern NCR townships. Capable of running at operational speeds of up to 160 km/h, RapidX cuts down travel time between Delhi (Anand Vihar/Sarai Kale Khan) and Meerut to under 55 minutes.

garhmukteshwar, situated conveniently along the major arterial corridor connected via 6-lane NH-9 and the newly opened Ganga Expressway interchange, sits right in the growth funnel of this mega transit system. As commuters can comfortably travel from Meerut or Ghaziabad transit hubs in under 30 minutes, garhmukteshwar effectively joins the daily commuting orbit of NCR working professionals seeking serene, green, and spacious living options.
