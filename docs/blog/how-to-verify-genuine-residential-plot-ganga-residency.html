2. Step-by-Step Checklist to Verify Genuine Plots

Execute this rigorous 5-step verification procedure before finalizing your plot purchase:

1
Inspect Original Title Deeds & Chain Documents

Examine the Sale Deed (Kraya Patra), Gift Deeds, or Partition Deeds to verify that the seller holds undisputed legal ownership of the specific land parcel.

2
Obtain and Verify the Encumbrance Certificate (EC)

An EC from the local sub-registrar office confirms that the property is free from any legal monetary liabilities, pending mortgages, or court attachments.

3
Verify Khatauni and Land Revenue Records

Cross-check land records on UP revenue portals (Bhulekh UP) to confirm agricultural-to-residential land conversion status and actual plot measurements.

4
Examine Township Zoning & Master Plan

Ensure the township layout complies with regional planning norms. You can view our planned layout on the Master Plan Page.

5
Confirm On-Site Infrastructure Development

Inspect physical amenities like boundary walls, internal wide roads, and electrical poles as highlighted on our Amenities Page.
