1. Legal Framework: Can NRIs Purchase Plots in India?

One of the most common queries among global buyers concerns ownership eligibility. Under the Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA) regulations set by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI):

  • Full Eligibility: NRIs and OCIs are permitted to acquire residential and commercial plots in India freely.
  • No RBI Approval Required: You do not need any prior special clearance or notification sent to the RBI for purchasing standard residential land parcels.
  • Restriction on Agricultural Land: While outright agricultural land or plantation property purchases are restricted for general NRIs, structured plotted developments like Ganga Residency feature approved residential land use, making them entirely legal and hassle-free.

3. Financial Transactions: NRE vs. NRO Accounts

Managing remittances is crucial for smooth property acquisition. Under Indian tax and foreign exchange laws, payments for property purchases can be completed through:

  • NRE (Non-Resident External) Account: Funds held in NRE accounts are fully repatriable (both principal and rental or capital gains returns can be freely transferred back abroad). Payments made directly from foreign inward remittances or NRE accounts facilitate smooth capital repatriation.
  • NRO (Non-Resident Ordinary) Account: Funds in NRO accounts represent income earned within India (such as rentals or local investments). These can also be utilized for domestic property payments, though repatriation limits apply as per current regulations.
