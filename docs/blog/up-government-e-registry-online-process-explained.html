1. Understanding the UP IGRS Portal and Legal Framework

The Stamps and Registration Department of Uttar Pradesh operates the official IGRS UP portal (Stamp and Registration Department, Government of UP). This digital ecosystem allows property buyers and developers to execute deeds online, calculate precise circle rates, pay stamp duty digitally, and schedule sub-registrar appointments without bureaucratic friction.

For buyers looking at plots near the Ganga Expressway, having clear documentation and complying with UP land norms guarantees long-term asset security and effortless resale value.

2. Step-by-Step Breakdown of the UP E-Registry Workflow

Navigating the online property mutation and registration process involves several structured stages. Follow this roadmap to ensure a seamless experience at Ganga Residency:

1
User Registration on IGRS UP Portal

Create a secure profile on the official UP Stamps & Registration portal using your mobile number, email, and ID credentials to access online deed drafting services.

2
Deed Drafting & Property Details Entry

Input exact property specifications, including plot number, area dimensions, sector details from our Master Plan, and buyer/seller identities.

3
Stamp Duty & Registration Fee Calculation

The system automatically computes the mandatory stamp duty (typically 7% for men, 6% for women, or joint concessions) and registration fees based on prevailing circle rates.

4
Online Payment & Slot Booking

Pay stamp duty securely via net banking or authorized payment gateways, and select an available date and time slot for physical verification at the local sub-registrar office.

5
Biometric Verification & Final Deed Execution

Visit the sub-registrar office with two witnesses and required identity proofs for biometric thumb impressions, photograph capture, and final deed signing.

3. Essential Documents Required for Plot Registration in UP

To ensure your e-registry application is approved on the first submission without delays, keep the following documentation ready:

Buyer & Seller Identity Proofs: Aadhaar Cards, PAN Cards, passport-size photographs, and address verification documents for all applicants.

Property Title Documents: Approved layout sanction, chain of previous ownership deeds, builder-buyer agreement, and latest property tax receipts.

Payment Receipts: Challans or digital payment acknowledgments confirming full payment of stamp duty and registration fees through the IGRS portal.
