1. Understanding Ground Coverage and FAR Limits

Ground coverage and Floor Area Ratio (FAR) dictate the maximum permissible built-up area relative to your plot size. In plotted developments like Ganga Residency, these limits prevent overcrowding, ensure adequate light penetration, and preserve open community spaces.

Ground Coverage

Typically restricted to 65% to 75% of the total plot area, leaving the remaining space open for ventilation, pathways, and green landscaping.

FAR & Vertical Expansion

Allows multi-level vertical construction up to Stater + 2 or G+2 floors depending on plot dimensions, supporting joint family living or rental units.

Maximum Height Caps

Stricter height ceilings maintain uniformity across sectors, protecting neighborhood skyline aesthetics and privacy across neighboring plots.

For buyers evaluating whether to invest in compact 100 sq. yd. parcels or expansive 150+ sq. yd. plots, understanding how these ratios apply across different dimensions is crucial. You can review additional layout strategies in our guide on 100 Sq. Yd. vs 150 Sq. Yd. Plots at Ganga Residency.

2. Mandatory Setback Regulations: Front, Rear, and Sides

Setbacks are the prescribed open spaces that must be left unconstructed around the perimeter of your building. They are non-negotiable legal requirements enforced to guarantee fire engine access, natural light circulation, and absolute privacy between adjacent homes.

At Ganga Residency, setback requirements vary slightly based on plot orientation and street width:

1
Front Setback

Essential for parking, vehicle maneuvering, and maintaining distance from internal township roads. Usually ranges from 5 to 8 feet depending on road width.

2
Rear Setback

Mandatory backyard clearance (typically 3 to 5 feet) dedicated to utility placement, drainage access, and cross-ventilation for ground-floor living areas.

3
Side Setbacks

Applicable primarily for corner plots or semi-detached units to ensure light shafts and window clearance on side facades.
