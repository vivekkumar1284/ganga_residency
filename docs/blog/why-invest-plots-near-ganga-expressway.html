Nearby Cultural & Eco Landmarks

Living at Ganga Residency offers a serene getaway surrounded by sacred heritage and natural landscapes along the holy Ganges riverbank.

  • Brijghat Holy Ghats: Located just a short drive away, providing picturesque riverside promenades and cultural heritage centers.
  • Eco-Tourism Trails: Clean, green surroundings ideal for morning walks, bird watching, and pollution-free weekend relaxation.

Schools & Higher Education Proximity

For families planning to construct permanent homes, access to reputable schools and colleges is available within a 5 to 15-minute radius:

  • GS International School: Modern CBSE curriculum school with bus routes servicing the area.
  • Royal School & Regional Colleges: Established educational institutions offering higher secondary and degree programs.

Civic Infrastructure & Emergency Facilities

Safety and essential everyday convenience are well-integrated into the local ecosystem:

  • 24/7 Security & Local Police Station: Immediate access to local administrative security posts.
  • Fuel & EV Charging Outlets: Multiple petrol pumps and EV fast-charging stations located along the highway route.
  • Banking & ATMs: Major public and private sector banks operating within close proximity.

Internal Township Amenities Breakdown

Inside the gated compound of Ganga Residency, residents enjoy a range of master-planned facilities:
