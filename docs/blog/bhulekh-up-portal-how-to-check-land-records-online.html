1. What is the Bhulekh UP Portal and Why Does it Matter?

'Bhulekh' is a portmanteau of Bhumi (Land) and Lekh (Account). The Department of Revenue in Uttar Pradesh digitized all land records to eliminate fraudulent transactions, intermediaries, and title disputes. For anyone eyeing residential plots near Ganga Expressway, checking the Bhulekh database is the first line of defense in verifying that the land is legally converted, free of encumbrances, and registered under valid titles.

2. Step-by-Step Procedure to Check Land Records on Upbhulekh

Accessing public land records in Uttar Pradesh is entirely digital and free of cost. Follow these structured steps to check records for properties in Hapur district, Garhmukteshwar, and surrounding regions:

1
Visit the Official Portal

Open your web browser and navigate to the official website: upbhulekh.gov.in. Ensure you use the secure government portal to avoid phishing sites.

2
Select 'Khatauni Nakal' (Record of Rights)

On the homepage menu, click on the option titled 'उद्धरण देखें' (View Khatauni/Record of Rights). You will be prompted to enter a simple captcha verification code for security.

3
Choose Administrative Jurisdiction

Select your region hierarchically: District (जनपद) -> Tehsil (तहसील) -> Village (ग्राम). For Garhmukteshwar properties, select Hapur district followed by Garhmukteshwar tehsil and the specific village name (such as Salarpur or surrounding areas).

4
Search by Desired Parameter

You can search the records using multiple filters: Khasra/Gata Number, Khata Number, Owner's Name (First/Last name in Hindi), or Date of Transfer.

5
View and Validate Record

Click search to view the digital copy of the Khatauni. Verify ownership names, share percentages, and ensure there are no active court stay orders or disputed encumbrances.

3. Key Terminology to Know on the Bhulekh UP Portal

Navigating government land portals requires familiarity with standard revenue terminology. Understanding these terms prevents confusion during legal audits:

Revenue Term Hindi Term Definition & Importance for Buyers
Khatauni खतौनी A legal document containing details of all land parcels owned by a specific person or family, including survey numbers and total area.
Khasra खसरा A specific parcel number assigned to a demarcated piece of land on the village revenue map (Shajra). Essential for pinpointing plot boundaries.
Mutation (Dakhil Kharij) दाखल खारिज The process of transferring land ownership in government records from the seller to the buyer after a sale deed is registered.
Gata / Plot गाटा संख्या The unique revenue survey number designated to an undivided agricultural or converted land block before township plotting.

5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) on UP Land Records

Q: Is the online Khatauni downloaded from Bhulekh legally admissible in court?
A: The online copy serves as an instant reference for due diligence. However, for legal proceedings or bank loan submissions, obtaining a digitally signed or certified copy from the Tehsil office is recommended.

Q: Can I check land records of any district in Uttar Pradesh?
A: Yes, the portal covers all 75 districts of Uttar Pradesh, allowing you to search records across any tehsil and village.
