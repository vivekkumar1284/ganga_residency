Is a 15-Acre Plotted Township a Good Investment? What to Check

Planned internal layout & modern infrastructure at Ganga Residency Garhmukteshwar

When evaluating plotted land investments in expanding belts like Garhmukteshwar, scale matters. A **15-acre gated township** offers the ideal sweet spot: large enough to deliver premium amenities and wide roads, yet manageable enough for rapid delivery and immediate habitation.

As seen in projects like Ganga Residency, investing in a mid-sized integrated township offers superior capital appreciation compared to isolated standalone plots. However, buyers must verify key parameters before making a commitment.

1. Why 15 Acres is the "Sweet Spot" Scale for Plotted Developments

Very large townships (50+ acres) often suffer from prolonged development cycles spanning over a decade. Conversely, small 2-acre layouts lack space for parks, security gates, and wide internal roads. A 15-acre footprint balances speed with luxury.

Gated Community Security

15-acre layouts allow centralized boundary walling, 24/7 manned entry gates, and full CCTV monitoring for safety.

Superior Road Ratios

Provides ample space for 30ft to 40ft wide internal paved roads, footpaths, and underground utility lines.

Dedicated Green Belts

Ensures at least 10–15% open space allocated for central landscaped parks, jogging tracks, and playgrounds.

Underground Infrastructure

Integrated electrical conduits, water pipelines, and storm water drainage systems planned from day one.

2. Scale Comparison: 15-Acre Township vs. Small Standalone Layouts

Here is how a planned 15-acre gated township compares with small unorganized colony plots:

Feature / Parameter 15-Acre Gated Township (e.g. Ganga Residency) Small Isolated / Standalone Plots
Security & Privacy Gated entry, perimeter wall & 24/7 security Open access, no perimeter security
Road Widths 30 ft. to 40 ft. paved wide roads Narrow 15 ft. to 20 ft. unpaved lanes
Lifestyle Amenities Parks, play areas, green belts, streetlights None; zero dedicated open spaces
Resale Value & Demand High demand from families & high ROI Limited resale liquidity & slower growth
Maintenance Organized RWA maintenance management Unorganized, self-managed civic upkeep

2

5
