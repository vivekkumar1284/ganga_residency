5-Stage Post-Booking Operational Journey

To ensure 100% peace of mind, we have standardized our post-sale workflow into five distinct, trackable stages. Here is exactly what happens behind the scenes and what you will receive after securing your plot at Ganga Residency:

  • 1

    Instant Payment Receipt & Allotment Letter Issuance

    Within 24 to 48 hours of paying your token booking amount, you receive an official, stamped Payment Receipt along with a provisional Plot Allotment Letter specifying your exact plot number, dimensions, facing, and total consideration. Check our Master Plan to understand plot layouts and orientation.

  • 2

    KYS (Know Your Customer) & Legal Document Verification

    Our legal documentation team cross-verifies your submitted ID proof, PAN card, address credentials, and passport-size photographs to draft flawless sale agreements. You will receive a complete set of title deed photocopies, Khasra details, and Section 143 agricultural-to-residential land conversion certificates for your personal records or bank loan processing.

  • 3

    Flexible Payment Schedule Updates & Reminders

    Whether you opted for a down-payment discount or a flexible construction-linked installment plan, our CRM desk provides digital account statements after every transaction. No hidden charges or unexpected surprises—every detail aligns with the published Ganga Residency Price List.

  • 4

    Physical Ground Demarcation & Site Boundary Pillar Fitting

    Once 50% or full payment is completed according to your chosen plan, our civil engineering team coordinates a formal joint site visit. Concrete boundary pillars marked with your assigned plot number are fixed right in your presence using precision DGPS surveying equipment.

  • 5

    Legal Sale Deed Registration & Possession Handover

    Our team schedules your appointment at the local Sub-Registrar Office in garhmukteshwar. We prepare the stamp paper, draft the final Sale Deed, assist with thumb impression procedures, and hand over key physical possession documents.
