2. Plot Loans vs. Home Loans: Key Structural Differences

A common pitfall for first-time buyers is assuming that land purchase loans operate identically to standard home construction loans. Recognizing these distinctions is vital for accurate financial modeling:

Loan-to-Value (LTV) Ratio

Plot loans typically finance up to 70% to 75% of the plot's registered value, requiring a slightly higher down payment compared to constructed apartments.

Interest Rate Spread

Plot loan interest rates often carry a minor premium (0.25% to 0.50% higher) over standard ready-to-move housing loans due to land valuation risks.

Construction Clause

Many banks mandate that residential construction on the purchased plot must commence within 3 to 5 years of loan disbursal to retain specific tax benefits.
