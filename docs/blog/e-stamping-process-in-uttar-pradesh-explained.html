1. What is E-Stamping and Why is it Mandatory in Uttar Pradesh?

E-Stamping (Electronic Stamping) is a secure computer-based application introduced by the Government of India to facilitate hassle-free payment of stamp duty on property transactions, agreements, and deeds. In Uttar Pradesh, the Department of Stamps and Registration governs all real estate transactions under the Indian Stamp Act.

Fraud Prevention

Unique Identification Numbers (UIN) and encrypted barcodes eliminate the risk of duplicate or counterfeit stamp papers.

Instant Verification

Any buyer or sub-registrar can instantly verify the authenticity of an e-stamp certificate online via the SHCIL portal.

Time-Saving Process

Generate stamp certificates within minutes without standing in long queues at traditional treasury offices.

Permanent Archiving

Digital records ensure your tax and duty payments remain safely archived and retrievable for future resale or legal audits.

2. Step-by-Step Guide to the E-Stamping Process in UP

Executing an e-stamp for your land purchase involves a systematic sequence of online and offline touchpoints. Follow this roadmap when finalizing your plot acquisition:

01
Calculate Required Stamp Duty

Determine the precise market value of the plot using the UP Stamp and Registration Department’s online valuation portal (IGRS UP) based on circle rates.

02
Access the SHCIL Portal or Authorized Center

Visit the official Stock Holding Corporation of India (SHCIL) website, select Uttar Pradesh as your state, and create your secure user profile.

03
Fill Out the Application Form

Enter exact buyer and seller details, description of the property, purpose of stamp duty (sale deed / conveyance deed), and payment denomination.

04
Make Online Payment

Pay the exact stamp duty amount securely via Net Banking, Debit/Credit Card, UPI, or NEFT/RTGS from your bank account.

05
Download & Print E-Stamp Certificate

Once processed, download the official e-stamp certificate containing the tamper-proof UIN, barcode, and transaction summary for registry submission.
