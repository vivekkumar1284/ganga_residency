1. Why Demarcation Matters in Real Estate Investments

In unorganized sector developments, land buyers often face conflicts regarding overlapping boundaries, displaced road widths, or inaccurate plot sizes. Boundary demarcation is the legal and technical process of locating, measuring, and physically marking the exact corner points of a specific plot based on approved revenue maps and layout plans.

At Ganga Residency garhmukteshwar, boundary demarcation protects plot owners in several essential ways:

  • Guaranteed Plot Dimensions: Eliminates discrepancies between the area mentioned in your registry documents and actual ground reality.
  • Zero Overlap Disputes: Laser-guided total station surveys establish clear line-of-sight boundaries between adjacent properties.
  • Immediate Construction Readiness: Physical corner pillars allow you to start boundary wall construction or villa development right away.
  • Hassle-Free Vastu Alignment: Helps owners easily align their building design with cardinal directions. Review our Vastu-friendly plot layout guide for planning tips.

2. The 5-Step Property Survey Process at Ganga Residency

Our engineering and surveying teams follow a standardized 5-step workflow for every plot delivered to buyers within the township:

Step 1: Revenue Boundary Verification

Engineers cross-reference the master township land record with UP Revenue Department Khasra maps to verify perimeter coordinates.

Step 2: DGPS Grid Alignment

Differential Global Positioning System (DGPS) tools map satellite coordinates to lock in fixed ground control points across wide township avenues.

Step 3: Total Station Corner Marking

Laser-reflecting electronic total stations measure plot lengths, widths, and diagonals to pinpoint four distinct corner coordinates.

Step 4: Pillar Demarcation & Possession

Concrete boundary pillars stamped with plot numbers are permanently embedded into the ground at each corner.

Following boundary physical marking, buyers receive an official Plot Demarcation Certificate alongside their possession documents. Before finalizing your booking, make sure to read our detailed checklist on legal documents to check before purchasing a residential plot.

4. Section 143 Non-Agricultural Approvals & Revenue Mapping

A crucial factor in land surveying is ensuring that agricultural land has been legally converted for residential use. Under UP revenue law, Section 143 conversion ensures that land is officially recognized for housing and non-agricultural activity.

7. Frequently Asked Questions

Can I re-measure my plot on-site during possession?

Yes! Our survey team will accompany you during physical possession with an electronic total station device to measure every boundary line in your presence.

What happens if a boundary pillar is damaged during construction?

Ganga Residency keeps digital DGPS coordinate backups for every plot. If a corner pillar is displaced during foundation excavation, our site engineer can re-mark the exact spot using master reference points.

Are internal roads demarcated separately from residential plots?

Yes. All 30-foot and 40-foot internal avenues are demarcated as separate township property, ensuring your full plot area remains untouched by future road maintenance.
