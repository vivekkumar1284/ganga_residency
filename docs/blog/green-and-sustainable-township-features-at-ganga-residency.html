Advanced Rainwater Harvesting & Groundwater Recharge

Water security is one of the defining challenges of modern real estate. garhmukteshwar benefits from abundant naturally high water tables due to its proximity to the Ganges, and Ganga Residency ensures this precious resource is continuously replenished through scientific water harvesting recharge pits.

The entire township features a graded storm-water drainage matrix that collects surface runoff during monsoon months. Instead of letting rainwater go to waste in polluted municipal drains, the water is filtered through dual-stage desilting chambers and directed into underground deep recharge wells.

  • Multi-Point Recharge Wells: Strategically positioned throughout the township layout to prevent waterlogging during heavy downpours.
  • Permeable Paving & Green Shoulders: Pedestrian walkways and roadside curbs utilize porous materials allowing direct ground absorption.
  • 24/7 Water Pressure Network: Hydro-pneumatic pumping systems ensure reliable, continuous water delivery across all residential sectors.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is solar power compulsory for individual houses at Ganga Residency?

Solar power is pre-installed across all township common areas and streetlights. For individual plot owners, solar panel installation is optional, but all electrical conduits and roof orientations are engineered to support rooftop solar systems easily.

How does the rainwater harvesting system operate?

The township utilizes deep groundwater recharge wells connected to specialized storm-water filtration drains. Rainwater from roads and common areas passes through silt traps before replenishing the underground aquifer.

Are the green belts and parks maintained by the township management?

Yes, all common parks, central green belts, boundary plantations, and streetlights are maintained by the central township facility management team.
