1. Demystifying GST on Plot Purchases

One of the most frequent inquiries from prospective real estate buyers is whether Goods and Services Tax (GST) applies to land transactions. Under current Indian tax regulations, the sale of raw or vacant land (bare land without any pre-existing construction) attracts 0% GST.

However, if the developer provides specialized commercial amenities, composite bundled packages, or if construction services are contracted simultaneously on the plot, varying tax rates can apply to those specific service components. It is vital to consult our advisors to verify the precise tax categorization of your chosen plot. 2. Mandatory Government Stamp Duty and Registration Fees

While the basic plot cost forms the primary investment, state government levies such as stamp duty and property registration charges are mandatory statutory expenses required to legally transfer ownership title to your name.

In Uttar Pradesh, stamp duty typically ranges between 5% to 7% of the property’s market value or circle rate (whichever is higher), with nominal concessions often applicable for female co-owners. Registration fees generally add another 1% to the total calculation.
